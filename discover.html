About MediCare Plus

Founded in 2005 by Dr. Arjun Adheera, MediCare Plus was created to redefine healthcare in Sri Lanka through innovation, compassion, and excellence. What began as a small practice has grown into one of the nation's most trusted healthcare networks.

At MediCare Plus, we focus on building trust, restoring hope, and empowering healthier lives with modern facilities, advanced technology, and skilled professionals delivering personalized, world-class care.

Our Vision

To be Sri Lanka's most trusted and innovative healthcare network, recognized for medical excellence, compassionate service, and community well-being.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, accessible, and compassionate healthcare through advanced medical expertise and modern facilities.

We are dedicated to:

  • Promoting health and wellness in every community we serve.
  • Empowering patients through education and preventive care.
  • Upholding integrity, empathy, and professionalism in all we do.

Our Story

Over the past two decades, MediCare Plus has evolved from a single clinic into a healthcare network offering specialized medical centers, laboratories, and digital health services.
